Power bar

Press anywhere on the bar, pull down to set power, release to shoot. Releasing in the top 8% cancels. 40 px wide on desktop and phone (hit area 48 px), full table height. Fill colour is the ramp at the fill’s leading edge: --power-low → --power-mid at 55% → --power-high.

Cancel

5 · In the cancel zone

Top 8% turns foul-red; the readout says “Cancel”; releasing here shoots nothing.

6 · Released: shoot

150 ms: brass ring flashes, fill collapses to the top (ease-in). Becomes the faint “last” fill.
